## Reading

The **Recursive Language Models** paper is the cleanest framing I''ve seen
for context rot — store intermediate state in a Python REPL environment
instead of the prompt. Pair it with the **Ramp Labs** finding that
autonomous agents systematically ignore their own budgets unless a separate
controller model gates spend, and you get the skeleton of how the next
generation of agentic workflows actually has to be built: working memory
outside the model, spend authority outside the model.

The macro tape was loud: 92K tech layoffs, Meta -10%, Anthropic at $1T vs
OAI at $880B, AWS putting $26B into Anthropic, Google putting $40B in.
Markets quietly shifted from P/E to FCF multiples and back to GAAP with
employee comp as a real expense — and sovereigns are issuing 40-year and
100-year bonds (Google''s 100-year was 10× oversubscribed).
